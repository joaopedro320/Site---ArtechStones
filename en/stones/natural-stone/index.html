Natural stone: granite, marble and quartzite

Artechstone's newest line brings together natural stone from Brazil, Spain, Mexico, India and other countries.

Chat on WhatsAppGet the catalogue

Granite forms as magma cools slowly deep underground, so every piece is unique, with its own colour and pattern. It resists abrasion, heat and many chemicals, making it a strong choice for kitchen and bathroom countertops, floors, cladding and facades.

Upkeep is simple: regular cleaning and an occasional sealant keep the surface glossy and impermeable. Marble and quartzite complete the line for projects that call for bold veining and a timeless look.

Granite, marble and quartzite

  • Every piece is unique
  • Resists abrasion and heat
  • Brazilian and international origin
  • Simple upkeep

Applications

  • Kitchen and bathroom countertops
  • Floors and cladding
  • Facades
  • Architecture and interior projects

Which natural stones does Artechstone sell and where do they come from?

Granite, marble and quartzite from Brazil, Spain, Mexico, India and other countries.

How do I choose the right material?

For kitchen and bathroom countertops, ArtechQuartzo. For outdoor areas, ArtechNano. For walls, furniture and panels, ArtechLâminas. For unique natural veining, granite, marble and quartzite. The team can help you choose for each space.
