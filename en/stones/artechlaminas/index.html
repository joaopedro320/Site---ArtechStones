ArtechLâminas, sintered ultra-compact slabs

Artechstone's hero product. Ultra-compact surfaces that reproduce a wide range of material styles, from classic marble to concrete.

Chat on WhatsAppGet the catalogue

New surface formats allow ARTECHSTONE® sintered ultra-compact slabs to reproduce a wide range of styles. As in nature, patterns vary, and each slab offers several faces that can be combined.

They are thin, high-strength sheets valued for their lightness and easy installation, and they suit modern, rustic and more sophisticated interiors.

Sintered ultra-compact slabs

  • High strength at a thin gauge
  • Lightweight and easy to install
  • Several combinable faces
  • Marble, stone and concrete looks

Applications

  • Wall cladding
  • Custom furniture
  • Decorative panels
  • Modern, rustic and refined interiors

What are ArtechLâminas?

ArtechLâminas are sintered ultra-compact slabs, Artechstone's hero product. Thin and high-strength, they reproduce marble, stone and concrete looks, offer several combinable faces and are used for wall cladding, custom furniture and decorative panels, with low weight and easy installation.

What is the difference between ArtechLâminas, ArtechQuartzo and ArtechNano?

ArtechQuartzo is for indoor use, with high resistance to stains and scratches, ideal for countertops. ArtechNano works indoors and outdoors, with near-zero liquid absorption. ArtechLâminas are thin slabs for walls, furniture and panels. Natural stone brings unique veining in every piece.

  • ArtechQuartzoQuartz
  • ArtechNanoCrystallised glass
  • Natural stoneGranite, marble and quartzite
