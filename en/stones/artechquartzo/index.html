ArtechQuartzo, quartz for countertops and cladding

A refined look with the texture and weight of natural stone, in a non-porous surface made for everyday use.

Chat on WhatsAppGet the catalogue

ArtechQuartzo is made with at least 90% natural raw material, including quartz crystals. Quartz is one of the four hardest natural minerals, which makes the surface resistant to scratches and heavy impact.

The non-porous surface resists stains from coffee, wine, lemon, olive oil, makeup and many other substances, and withstands heat without staining as long as it isn't exposed excessively. Colours come in three lines: Estrelar, glossy with a sparkle effect; Liso, with uniform colours; and Veios, which reproduces natural marble.

Quartz

  • Non-porous surface
  • Resists stains, heat, scratches and impact
  • Made to ISO 9000 standards
  • Estrelar, Liso and Veios lines

Applications

  • Kitchen countertops
  • Bathrooms
  • Floors and wall cladding
  • Staircases and offices

Frequently asked questions

See all questions

Which colours does ArtechQuartzo come in?

ArtechQuartzo colours are grouped into three lines: Estrelar, glossy with a sparkle effect; Liso, with uniform colours; and Veios, which reproduces the look of natural marble.

Do the surfaces resist coffee, wine and cleaning products?

Yes. ArtechQuartzo has a non-porous surface that resists coffee, wine, lemon, olive oil and makeup, and ArtechNano resists acids and alkalis with near-zero absorption.

Are the materials quality certified?

Yes. ArtechQuartzo and ArtechNano surfaces are manufactured in line with ISO 9000 quality standards.
