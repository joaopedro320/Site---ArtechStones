ArtechNano, uniform white inside and out

A crystallised, homogeneous surface in uniform white with an exceptional shine close to that of natural stone.

Chat on WhatsAppGet the catalogue

ArtechNano is made from glass crystals and white marble powder, melted in a furnace at around 1800 °C and cooled into slabs for fabrication.

The result is a dense, durable material that resists scratches, cracks and abrasion, acids and alkalis, impact, compression and bending, stains, yellowing, dirt, mould and bacteria, temperature changes, sun and rain. It doesn't chip or crack when cut or finished, and allows narrower joints on floors and walls.

Crystallised glass

  • Near-zero liquid absorption
  • Indoor and outdoor use
  • 1-year warranty
  • Made to ISO 9000 standards

Applications

  • Panels, cladding and facades
  • Floors, countertops and edges
  • Bathrooms, kitchens and outdoor kitchens
  • Stairs, fireplaces, gardens, furniture and sculpture

Frequently asked questions

Can ArtechNano be used outdoors?

Yes. ArtechNano is suitable for indoor and outdoor use, including facades, gardens, outdoor floors and outdoor kitchens, because it withstands sun, rain and temperature changes and has near-zero liquid absorption.

What warranty does ArtechNano have?

ArtechNano has a 1-year warranty. Warranty terms and technical documents for each material are sent by the sales team together with the quote.

Are the materials quality certified?

Yes. ArtechQuartzo and ArtechNano surfaces are manufactured in line with ISO 9000 quality standards.
