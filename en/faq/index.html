Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about our materials, the company and how to buy.

What is Artechstone?

Artechstone is a Brazilian importer and distributor of imported stone, founded in 2016 by Fabiane Braga and Colin B. Thomas. Its head office and warehouse are in Cachoeiro de Itapemirim (Espírito Santo) and its gallery is in São Paulo. It sells engineered stone (ArtechLâminas, ArtechQuartzo and ArtechNano) and natural stone (granite, marble and quartzite).

What are ArtechLâminas?

ArtechLâminas are sintered ultra-compact slabs, Artechstone's hero product. Thin and high-strength, they reproduce marble, stone and concrete looks, offer several combinable faces and are used for wall cladding, custom furniture and decorative panels, with low weight and easy installation.

What is the difference between ArtechLâminas, ArtechQuartzo and ArtechNano?

ArtechQuartzo is for indoor use, with high resistance to stains and scratches, ideal for countertops. ArtechNano works indoors and outdoors, with near-zero liquid absorption. ArtechLâminas are thin slabs for walls, furniture and panels. Natural stone brings unique veining in every piece.

Which colours does ArtechQuartzo come in?

ArtechQuartzo colours are grouped into three lines: Estrelar, glossy with a sparkle effect; Liso, with uniform colours; and Veios, which reproduces the look of natural marble.

Can ArtechNano be used outdoors?

Yes. ArtechNano is suitable for indoor and outdoor use, including facades, gardens, outdoor floors and outdoor kitchens, because it withstands sun, rain and temperature changes and has near-zero liquid absorption.

Are the materials quality certified?

Yes. ArtechQuartzo and ArtechNano surfaces are manufactured in line with ISO 9000 quality standards.

What warranty does ArtechNano have?

ArtechNano has a 1-year warranty. Warranty terms and technical documents for each material are sent by the sales team together with the quote.

Do the surfaces resist coffee, wine and cleaning products?

Yes. ArtechQuartzo has a non-porous surface that resists coffee, wine, lemon, olive oil and makeup, and ArtechNano resists acids and alkalis with near-zero absorption.

Which natural stones does Artechstone sell and where do they come from?

Granite, marble and quartzite from Brazil, Spain, Mexico, India and other countries.

Does Artechstone handle large-volume imports?

Yes. Through its network of manufacturers in China, Artechstone imports large quantities of stone and has a wide range of products available for import.

Who does Artechstone sell to?

Stone fabricators, stone companies, architects, interior designers, builders and professionals looking for high-end stone for their projects.

Where is Artechstone located?

The head office and warehouse are at Rua Joanna Payer, 66C, Boa Vista Aeroporto, Cachoeiro de Itapemirim, Espírito Santo. The São Paulo gallery is at Rua Marcello Muller, 546, Jardim Independência, São Paulo, CEP 03223-060, Brazil.

How do I request a catalogue, samples or a quote?

Request the catalogue on the Downloads page. Quotes and samples are requested via WhatsApp +55 28 3521-2416, phone +55 28 3028-2299 or email. Artechstone serves all of Brazil.

How do I choose the right material?

For kitchen and bathroom countertops, ArtechQuartzo. For outdoor areas, ArtechNano. For walls, furniture and panels, ArtechLâminas. For unique natural veining, granite, marble and quartzite. The team can help you choose for each space.
