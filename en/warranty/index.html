Warranty and quality

Each line has its own characteristics. Here is what we guarantee, how the materials are made and the care that keeps them looking right.

ArtechNano: 1-year warranty

ArtechNano comes with a 1-year warranty. To make a claim, contact the team with your order details and installation site.

Made to ISO 9000 standards

ArtechQuartzo and ArtechNano surfaces are produced with advanced equipment in line with ISO 9000 quality standards.

Technical documentation

Warranty terms and technical information for each material are sent by the sales team together with your quote. Ask for the documents for the stone you are specifying.

Care by line

ArtechQuartzo
Heat resistant and won't stain at high temperatures, as long as it isn't exposed excessively. The non-porous surface resists coffee, wine, lemon, olive oil and makeup.
ArtechNano
Suitable indoors and outdoors, with near-zero liquid absorption and resistance to sun, rain and temperature changes.
Natural stone
Regular cleaning and an occasional sealant keep granite glossy and impermeable.
