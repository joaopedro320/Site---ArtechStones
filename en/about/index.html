A selection standard shaped across three continents

Artechstone is a Brazilian importer and distributor of imported stone, with warehouses in Espírito Santo and São Paulo.

Our story

Our founder, Fabiane Braga, lived in Singapore for 27 years. That is where her career in the stone industry began, at one of the world's largest stone companies, selling across Dubai, Australia, China, Thailand, the Philippines, Vietnam, Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore and beyond.

She later decided to invest that global expertise back into her home country. She turned it into real benefits for stonemasons, architects, construction companies and professionals looking for reliability and variety when choosing stone for their projects.

In 2016 she partnered with British investor Colin B. Thomas and together they founded Artechstone. With over 40 years of combined entrepreneurial experience, they share one goal: to bring Brazil the highest quality, premium, internationally sourced stone the market has to offer.
